# Inspiration and competitors

The **Inspiration** page helps you find creative ideas to adapt to your brand. It never promises that a competitor ad performs well: it explains only the public signals that are actually observed.

## Explore

Explore reads a shared library of Meta ads. Filter by category, market and ad format. Opening Explore never starts a paid collection.

Labels are factual: **Strong signal**, **Top X% by reach**, **Running for N days**, **New**, **Watch** or **Limited data**. They never represent an advertiser's ROAS, CTR, CPA or revenue.

## Add a competitor

Brand analysis can propose **candidates**. A candidate is always labelled **Needs review**: an AI suggestion is not automatically a competitor.

From the **Competitors** tab, you can:

- confirm a candidate when it genuinely belongs in your niche;
- reject it;
- add a brand manually with its name and, when available, its domain, Meta page and aliases.

Adding or confirming a brand never starts a collection on its own.

When a verified-source logo is available, it appears with the brand. Otherwise, CreatAds shows its initials instead of a guessed image.

## See a competitor's ads

After confirmation, **See ads** opens a preflight with the market, cache state and your Ads Library quota. A fresh cache opens immediately with no new collection or additional consent.

When no fresh cache exists, explicitly choose **Start monitoring**. It may use an Ads Library search allowance, never image-generation credits. If an identical collection is already running, you join its state instead of starting another one. If it fails, you can retry from the same preflight.

## Use an inspiration

From an ad detail, you can save, hide or use it as an inspiration in Easy Mode or Pro Mode. None of these actions uses generation credits. Credits are calculated only when you launch a creation.

Source-brand elements remain excluded from the reproduction prompt.
